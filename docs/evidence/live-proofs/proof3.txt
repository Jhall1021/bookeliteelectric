  WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE RELEASE
    Reading a candidate back describes what it was BUILT WITH, not what the
    project currently says. So compareBuild comparing a candidate's settings
    against the approved ones is sound: the value it reads cannot be moved out
    from under it by a later settings change. The receipt binding does not need
    to bind anything further on this account.

  SCOPE
    One deployment, one project, one field changed and changed back. It
    establishes that this deployment's settings did not follow this project's
    buildCommand. It does not establish immutability of the whole record against
    every kind of project change, and it is not a claim about Vercel in general.
